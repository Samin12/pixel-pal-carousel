## 02 - CAPTURE (INTERIOR)

Start with a real question.

Orange emphasis: question

Write down one question buyers ask before they hire you.

Illustration: holding a cream pixel notebook and pencil, listening attentively; a large rounded speech bubble above the notebook shows the sample buyer question

Tip: Use their words. Then answer clearly.

## 03 - EXPLAIN (INTERIOR)

Give one useful answer.

Orange emphasis: useful

Explain the problem, show one example, and give one next step.

Illustration: arranging three clean cream pixel note cards on a desk, each separate and legible with labels: Problem, Example, Next step; mascot is standing to the right of the cards as a helpful teacher

Tip: Teach one thing people can use today.

## 04 - REPURPOSE (INTERIOR)

Turn it into three posts.

Orange emphasis: three

Film the answer. Turn the steps into a carousel. Pull one takeaway into a short post.

Illustration: standing proudly beside three distinct large pixel content tiles: a video tile with a play symbol, a stacked carousel tile, and a short text tile; label them Video, Carousel, Short post

Tip: Same idea. A different way to learn.

## 05 - ORGANIZE (INTERIOR)

Make the workflow visible.

Orange emphasis: workflow

Move each post through four stages. Give it one owner and a due date.

Illustration: moving one orange card on a clean four-column cream workflow board; the columns read Draft, Review, Ready, Published; each column has one generic blank note card and no metrics, app logos or account data

Tip: Approve the content before it goes live.

## 06 - INVITE (INTERIOR)

Orange emphasis: invitation

Connect the lesson to your service. Invite interested readers to book a conversation.
